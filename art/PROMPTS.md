# Prompts da arte gerada

Gerados no **Google Gemini (app web, modo Pro, no Chrome do usuário)** em 11–12/10/2026, numa única conversa ("Cursed Knight Sprite Sheet") para manter o estilo. Cada imagem foi baixada em tamanho original (JPEG) para `art/raw/iso/` e processada com `npm run art` (`tools/art.mjs`).

**Convenções** (ART_BIBLE + direção aprovada): sujeito, câmera, poses, estilo, paleta, fundo e uso em todo prompt. Folhas em grade (4x2 ou 4x4) sobre **magenta chapado #FF00FF**: o Gemini não entrega transparência real e o pipeline remove o magenta. O Gemini costuma desenhar linhas de grade mesmo pedindo que não: o pipeline as remove (`gridLines: true`). Pedir "no purple or pink" para não confundir o recorte. Personagens: linha de cima de frente (sudeste); o jogo espelha para o outro lado.

A arte anterior (traço chapado, vista 3/4) foi substituída quando a direção isométrica pintada foi aprovada; seus prompts estão no histórico do git.

## hero-sheet.jpg (CHAR-001/CHAR-002)
> Create an image: a sprite sheet for an isometric dark fantasy action RPG. Wide 16:9 image with exactly 8 poses of the SAME character in a grid of 4 columns and 2 rows, each pose centered in its own equal cell with generous empty margin, all at the same scale and with identical design. CHARACTER: the protagonist, a cursed knight: closed great helm with a narrow cross-shaped eye slit, battered dark steel plate armor with engraved aged-gold trim, a tattered deep crimson tabard and cape, a cracked aged-gold halo floating behind the helm, a long straight sword. CAMERA: isometric view from above (about 35 degrees down), full body visible. TOP ROW, the knight faces diagonally toward the camera and to the right (south-east): 1 idle stance, 2 running, 3 sword slash mid-swing, 4 dodge lunge low to the ground. BOTTOM ROW, the same knight faces diagonally away from the camera and to the right (north-east, back and cape visible): 5 idle, 6 running, 7 sword slash mid-swing, 8 dodge lunge. STYLE: highly detailed dark fantasy illustration with painted volumes combined with fine ink cross-hatching like a Gustave Dore engraving and dark seinen manga, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from the upper left, worn materials (scratched steel, old leather, torn cloth), mostly charcoal and blue-gray with selective deep crimson and aged gold accents. Bold readable silhouette at small size. BACKGROUND: perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF everywhere, no floor, no cast shadows, no grid lines, no text, no watermark, no purple or pink on the character. USE: in-game sprites, cut out automatically by removing the magenta.
