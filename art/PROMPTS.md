# Prompts da arte gerada

Gerados no **Google Gemini (app web, modo Pro)**, numa única conversa para manter o estilo. Cada imagem foi baixada em tamanho original (JPEG) para `art/raw/` e processada com `npm run art`.

Convenções (ART_BIBLE): sujeito, câmera, poses, estilo, paleta, fundo e uso em todo prompt. Sprites em grade 2x2 sobre **magenta chapado #FF00FF** (o Gemini não entrega transparência real; o pipeline remove o magenta). Pedir "sem roxo/rosa" quando houver vidro ou magia, para não confundir o recorte.

## player-knight-sheet.jpg (CHAR-001)
> Create an image: a 2D game sprite sheet for a dark fantasy top-down action roguelite. SUBJECT: the player character, a cursed knight with a closed great helm with a narrow eye slit, dark plate armor with aged-ivory highlights, a tattered wine-red tabard and short cape, a broken aged-gold halo floating behind the helm, and a straight longsword. LAYOUT: exactly 4 poses of the same knight in a 2x2 grid of four equal quadrants. Top-left: idle, standing, sword lowered. Top-right: running mid-stride. Bottom-left: mid-slash, sword swung horizontally forward. Bottom-right: dodge, crouched low and lunging forward. Every pose faces right, is centered in its own quadrant with generous empty margin around it, and has the same scale and identical design. CAMERA: 3/4 top-down view (high camera looking down about 45 degrees), full body visible. STYLE: medieval woodcut and copperplate engraving mixed with gothic illustration and seinen manga inking: bold black contours, fine cross-hatching, no soft gradients. Bold readable silhouette that stays clear when shrunk to 64 pixels tall; moderate hatching, not noisy. PALETTE: only charcoal black, aged ivory, wine red and aged gold. BACKGROUND: perfectly flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, no gradient, no floor, no ground shadow, no grid lines, no borders, no text, no watermark. USE: in-game sprites that will be cut out automatically by removing the magenta.

Edição aplicada em seguida (versão usada):
> Edit the image you just made. Keep everything identical (same 2x2 layout, same knight design, same poses, same scale, same flat magenta #FF00FF background), with one fix: in the top-right running pose and in the bottom-right dodge pose the knight must hold the same straight longsword in his right hand, blade pointing backward and slightly down, fully visible. No other changes, no text, no watermark.
